# v0.4.1 — longer, more triumphant elapsed-turn endings

Longer-turn variants now add 0.8 seconds instead of only changing the voicing.
They retain the familiar opening, add two rising notes, and finish with a wider
chord that rings out. The six variants last 1.75–1.92 seconds, at matched RMS level.

The change applies to `done`, `answer`, `verdict`, `plan`, `artifact`, and
`published` when the existing optional elapsed-time treatment is enabled.
Normal cues, handoffs, caveats, failures, and in-flight behavior remain unchanged.
Each state's `long_turn` table exposes editable `lift` and `chord` notes.

Audition with `awaitonal play done --long-turn`, or enable the treatment with
`[notifications] long_turn_seconds = 120` in your service configuration.
See [validation](../../VALIDATION.md) for the 59 passing focused tests and
measured audio properties.
