# v0.4.2 — preserve notifications during expensive classification

A large reply could keep the listener busy long enough to expire a second accepted
connection before reading its already-arrived notification. This was a scheduling
race exposed by the Linux CI responsiveness check.

The listener now finishes bounded nonblocking socket reads and expiry handling
before doing classification work. Complete large messages are drained without
letting an earlier classification consume their intake window. Incomplete clients,
message-size limits, peer validation, queue limits, and notification priorities
retain their existing behavior.

This update includes the longer, more triumphant elapsed-turn endings from v0.4.1.
No audio or classification semantics change in this fix.
